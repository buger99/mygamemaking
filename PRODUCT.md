## Platform

web

## Users
The maker uses these pages in class and in presentations: the games are shown to and played by classmates or an audience. Play happens mainly on phones (portrait, touch).

## Product Purpose
`mygamemaking` is a collection of small single-file browser games and pages built while learning to make games. `rps.html` is a rock-paper-scissors game against the computer: exactly 5 rounds (draws and timeouts count as rounds); whoever wins more rounds wins, equal wins is a draw. Success means a player understands it instantly, enjoys a few rounds, and the maker can show it off proudly.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- `rps.html` features to preserve: three hand buttons (가위 ✌️ / 바위 ✊ / 보 ✋), a computer that picks at random, a round result showing both hands plus 이겼다 / 졌다 / 비겼다, scoreboard with a ROUND n/5 counter, a final win/lose/draw screen with 다시 하기, a win-streak counter with fire banners from 3 in a row and a best record, a chatty computer with random lines, speed mode (3-second countdown, timeout counts as a loss), 8-bit Web Audio sound effects with a mute toggle, confetti on wins, and a shake on losses.
- Plain static HTML/CSS/JS, one file per page, no build step. The local pixel font `fonts/Galmuri11.woff2` (OFL) is available.
- Also published as a private claude.ai artifact.
- All UI copy is Korean.

## Brand Commitments
- Must not feel too dark or scary: it can be retro, but it stays bright and fun.

## Evidence on Hand
- No real testimonials, users, or scores exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Instantly playable: the three choices are the most obvious thing on screen.
- Every outcome gets a clear, celebratory or comic reaction.
- Readable from a classroom distance and on a small phone alike.
